Company Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave every calendar year.

Employees can request annual leave through the company's internal leave management system.

Leave requests should normally be submitted at least 5 working days before the planned leave.

Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused leave days to the following year.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave. Employees should inform their manager as soon as possible when they are unable to work due to illness.

Employees who have completed at least 6 months of continuous employment are eligible for the standard annual leave allowance.

Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving leave requests.

For urgent leave situations, employees should contact their manager directly.
